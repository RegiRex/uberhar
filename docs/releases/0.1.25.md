# Uberhar 0.1.25 — bounded vertex evidence and offline replay

<!-- CodexAstraLocal: Diagnose the two reproduced Combo symptoms after the narrow 0.1.24 guard rejected no title batches; this release is not a rendering or performance fix. -->

The cold-cache Thor test of 0.1.24 still shows moon segments flashing and separate
ghost corruption. Its output-default guard rejected none of 773,137 examined
batches. This candidate adds an optional diagnostic to capture immutable data
from selected, successfully submitted Combo vertex draws and replay it offline.
Draw admission, shader generation and Native execution remain unchanged.

Capture is disabled by default. A strict, title-specific 8 KiB sidecar and an
explicit Automatic-to-Gameplay Test phase transition enable one finite window.
Discovery records at most 128 rows; selection retains shader identities, target
guards and the global per-swap optional-request ordinal. An unmatched or failed
attempt is censored rather than replaced by a different draw. Swap intervals are
not guest/video frame identifiers. The same APK supports discovery and subsequent
selected captures, without repeated diagnostic builds.

## Ownership and limits

<!-- CodexAstraLocal: State aggregate and per-packet caps, immutable lifetime ownership, disabled-path behavior and storage limitations precisely. -->

At most eight packets share a 4 MiB aggregate budget, with 3.75 MiB for raw
payload and 256 KiB reserved for fixed metadata and formatting. Each packet has
at most 496 KiB payload and 16 KiB serialized metadata. Discovery examines no more
than 1,024 optional attempts per swap; selection makes at most 32 attempts and
copies at most 8 MiB of raw payload over its lifetime, including abandoned
attempts and semantic UBO copies. Count and indexed span are each limited to
4,096. Fixed typed metadata and JSON/stdio work have separate bounds.

The snapshot owns uploaded vertices, ordered indices, fixed/default attributes,
program/swizzle words and actual bound uniform semantic bytes. Intended PICA
uniforms are retained separately. Recording, queue acceptance and GPU completion
are distinct states. A small shared owner survives queued work; no new worker,
GPU wait or periodic logger is added. The disabled draw path constructs no
capture token. Native and Custom never load the sidecar.

Normal exit writes one bounded artifact with exclusive creation, preserving any
existing file. A killed process can lose evidence. The Android Uberhar flavor's
raw filesystem provider is supported; unsupported document-only providers reject
capture at initialization without affecting play. Android storage and submission
behavior still need real-device validation. Raw game payloads remain private in
ignored device-test artifacts and are never release assets.

## Replay and validation

<!-- CodexAstraLocal: Distinguish actual producer serialization, modeled host plumbing and synthetic GPU execution from real Thor evidence. -->

The host tool uses the production interpreter, input/output conversion and
draw-local register carry with the 64-entry FIFO. Mesa runs generated GLSL with
the captured typed uploads and actual bound vertex uniforms. Input-fetch,
actual-versus-intended uniform and fresh-register counterfactual comparisons are
reported separately. Replay ends before primitive correction, fragments and
rasterization; it cannot establish Adreno parity or a visible glitch's cause.

Independent source review found no remaining blocker. The actual production
Session harness passed 403 checks with modeled file/mapping plumbing; all 13
produced artifacts passed the independent protocol reader, including indexed
full-layout reconstruction. Large-capacity discovery and eight-packet fixtures measured
85,104 and 85,314 bytes against the 128 KiB cap. Independent replay passed 69
checks across 13 synthetic production CPU/Mesa cases. Native core/Vulkan/tests/room build, all host probes and 163 policy checks passed.
CTest passed 61 distinct cases and 1,096 assertions, with five existing
firmware-dependent skips and zero failures. Android, signing and publication
checks remain separate gates; see the
[progress record](../UBERHAR_PROGRESS.md) for current status.

Follow the [private capture guide](../../tools/uberhar/VERTEX_CAPTURE.md). Every
Dark Moon opening still resets File 1 and deletes its title Vulkan cache. Use
full Combo, Vulkan, 2x and the normal 100% limit; observe moon flashing across
consecutive frames separately from matched ghost corruption. Capture timings are
diagnostic, not performance qualification. The 0.2.0 target remains unqualified.
