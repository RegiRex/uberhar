# Uberhar 0.1.37 — cheaper ordered CPU batches

<!-- CodexAstraLocal: Describe the candidate's concrete work reduction and its
limits; private host timings do not establish a Thor or complete-title gain. -->

Native and Combo retain the same certified vertex arithmetic and ordered draws,
while reducing coordination and assembly work. On supported Linux/Android hosts,
persistent workers sleep on private futex words instead of sharing the dispatch
mutex. The owner still shades useful grains and joins every participant before
submitting results. Unsupported or restricted startup environments select the
portable condition-variable path. Neither transport spins to increase CPU use.
A permanent transport error after startup terminates safely rather than returning
while a worker still borrows job data; this is not a graceful recovery mechanism.

For admitted accelerated List/Shader draws, ordered assembly borrows immutable
vertices and avoids redundant intermediate first/second-vertex copies. It retains
exact winding, cross-draw tails, buffered state and callback-exception behavior.
Strip/Fan and ordinary scalar callers keep their existing assembly semantics.
An actual-rasterizer capability guard keeps software submission serial because
its framebuffer writes can alias later vertex inputs.

The ordinary parallel report identifies the selected waiting transport and samples
one completed admitted draw per 257. It separates FIFO planning, the worker-pool
call and ordered submission, with nested owner-work/join wall durations and exact
sample populations. These clocks exclude draw preparation and other frame work;
periodic samples are diagnostic evidence, not a scaled whole-run budget.

Finite regression checks cover actual pool scheduling, floating state, wake/wait
failures, fallback selection, borrowed-job lifetime, exact FIFO results and
persistent assembly state. Merged native compilation and CTest pass with five
existing firmware-dependent skips. The ordered-batch gate covers 3,744 draws;
504 focused assembly cases and two deliberate state defects also pass. A merged
actual-input/JIT/FIFO/hardware-output check matches all 112 complete vector/state
comparisons across 56 cohorts. Independent architecture and cleanliness reviews
found no blocker within these stated contracts. Production shader, ARM64, Android and signing gates
remain mandatory. The separate ARM64 narrow memory-return correction and general
Calculated renderer development remain private work, outside this candidate.

This is an experimental alpha. The previous build still averaged about 30% normal
speed in the two bounded Dark Moon openings. This candidate does not claim
99% speed, sustained all-core utilization or 4x qualification. One cold Native
and one full Combo Vulkan 2x opening will establish its device evidence after
all release gates pass. The full architecture and separate cleanliness review
are due after those tests.
